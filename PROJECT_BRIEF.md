Recruiters, hiring managers, analysts, and technical stakeholders hiring for:

- Healthcare Data Analyst

## Positioning

**Healthcare analytics grounded in mathematics, technology, and frontline operations.**

Alec is not presenting himself as a long-tenured data scientist. The strength of the story is the combination of formal quantitative training, Epic healthcare-systems experience, frontline operational context, and a portfolio that shows practical analytical reasoning.

## Desired recruiter takeaway

“Alec understands healthcare systems and operations, can work with data and models, communicates clearly, and has a credible bridge into an analyst role.”

## Core narrative

1. Applied Mathematics created the quantitative foundation.
2. Epic provided technical healthcare-systems and data-quality experience.
3. Red Cross provided frontline clinical operations, workflow, training, and constrained-resource decision-making.
4. Projects demonstrate SQL, Python, Power BI, machine learning, dashboarding, and business communication.

## Tone

- confident but not inflated
- technically literate but understandable
- candid about limitations
- practical and decision-oriented
- warm enough to feel human
